# CrowHunt

Description:
CrowHunt is endless point-and-click shooter game where the player try to gather as much points as possible while the crow fly to the player.

Gameplay:
Gather points by killing the crows. The more you kill the crows, the faster they get. You will lose when you lose all your health after enough crows fly by you.

Made in Godot with gdScripts.
